## Coverage and limits

GT team resolver covers 14 teams by explicit team name only, within GT-class standings. Manufacturer tables are excluded. Driver names alone never infer team identity. Unknown teams retain their text; do not substitute a manufacturer as though it were a team crest. Official IMSA logos for several other teams still had opaque backplates; omitted instead of introducing more opaque rectangles.

## Driver portraits

No portrait is approved by this review. Current-year official availability does not establish reuse or personality-rights clearance. Use a neutral silhouette until an individually licensed/authorized asset is documented with person, season, source, license, attribution and scope. Do not treat a photo of a past season as a current-year headshot.

- Formula 1 guidelines explicitly require rights-holder consent for reproduction of still images: https://www.formula1.com/en/information/guidelines.4EOKE9RRqevL4niTK9kWyt
- F1 legal notice limits site content reuse: https://www.formula1.com/en/information/legal-notices.7egvZU48hzrypubGBNcQKt
- WEC website copyright notice excludes public distribution: https://www.fiawec.com/en/page/mentions-legales-1
- WEC press conditions have a qualified editorial license and instruct users to check permissions with the photographer; this is not an unrestricted commercial/profile-image license: https://press.fiawec.com/Default/terms
