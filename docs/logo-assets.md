# Logo asset sources

IMSA, Formula E, NLS and Mercedes use the owner-supplied images from 2026-10-30. Original bytes are preserved in base64 modules; SVG viewports frame the supplied artwork without redrawing it.

Other manufacturer marks: Simple Icons (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0 icon artwork; trademarks remain with their respective owners. Match logos to team/manufacturer names from the standings, never to a driver by an assumed roster. Unmatched entries retain their text.

Alpine, Genesis, Jaguar and Cupra assets: https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos (original SVG marks).

2026-10-30 update: owner-supplied McLaren (IMG_7137.png), Racing Bulls (IMG_7138.jpeg), Red Bull (IMG_7134.png) and Williams (IMG_7135.jpeg). Original bytes are preserved; SVG viewports exclude Red Bull text and the Racing Bulls photo badge.
Haas: official https://www.haasf1team.com/themes/haas/assets/logos/new/TGRHF1_Team_Logo_Full_Vert.svg . Lexus: https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos/blob/main/lexus-logo.png . Marks remain trademarks of their owners.
All standings rows reserve the same 52px logo column. Shape-specific image sizes do not affect text alignment. Manufacturer coverage is tested against published IMSA GTP, GTD PRO and GTD manufacturer names; driver rows without published affiliations are not assigned invented teams.
